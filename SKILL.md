# XXD Panel 029 · 横向色域蜡粉笔手绘

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished relaxed wax-pastel drawing on rough ivory handmade paper. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must keep the same source's core proportion, gesture, direction, and defining cues while simplifying it into light matte wax-pastel marks with irregular, broken, friction-rich edges. One low-saturation horizontal paper-colour field, derived from the source's composite colour and emotion, relates subject, weight, and surrounding space. Offset placement, crop, or a source-earned crossing of the field boundary creates quiet modern rhythm; double-paper depth, restrained ageing, Risograph grain, and soft imperfect handwriting keep the result tactile rather than digitally smooth.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, action, function, and emotion through at least three source-specific cues while using one source-derived horizontal low-saturation paper field and one simplified light matte wax-pastel subject. The subject may be offset, cropped, or cross the colour-field boundary according to source gesture; field, subject, and upper/lower whitespace must form a deliberate proportion. It is not tracing, a small 10–20% book-cover vignette, acrylic colour blocks, children's crayon cartoon, cheap journal page, dense scenery, glossy vector art, photoreal painting, or 3D.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 029 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve identity, proportion, gesture, direction, action, function, emotion, and at least three defining cues → derive one low-saturation paper colour from the source's composite palette and feeling → extend it horizontally as a single quiet field whose height and position answer the subject's weight → simplify the subject into light matte wax-pastel mass and marks with irregular, broken, rubbed edges → place, crop, or let it cross the field boundary according to the source rather than centring by default → balance horizontal field, subject scale, positive/negative shape, and upper/lower ivory whitespace → reveal rough fibre, double-paper depth, restrained ageing, and Risograph grain → weave one short soft imperfect handwritten line along the field edge, subject gesture, or open paper**.

The childlike ease comes from relaxed reduction, wax resistance, broken friction, source-led imbalance, and recognisable identity—not from stock faces, mascots, suns, stars, decorative doodles, tape, stickers, or scrapbook props. Handmade paper, wax pastel, Risograph imperfection, vintage editorial illustration, and modern independent publishing are material influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the subject gesture, horizontal field proportion, boundary crossing, source-derived colour, wax mark rhythm, or handwriting relation. Also reject fixed beige/pink/blue templates, a tiny isolated vignette, full-frame colour blocks, busy backgrounds, evenly scattered doodles, opaque acrylic fills, smooth vector polish, commercial-cartoon styling, photoreal shading, complex shadows, 3D volume, and decorative journal symbols not earned by the source.

## 029 visual system

### Recognisable subject in relaxed wax pastel

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Simplify the principal subject or inseparable relation into light matte wax-pastel masses and marks. Remove busy detail while preserving core proportion, contour flow, pose, direction, action, function, and emotion at first glance.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. Any secondary mark must help identify it or minimally imply setting; it may not become a second focus or scrapbook decoration.

### One horizontal source-colour paper field

- Place exactly one horizontally extended low-saturation paper-colour field on rough ivory stock. Derive its hue, temperature, and value from the source's composite colour and emotional atmosphere; never impose a fixed hue.
- Let source weight, direction, and silhouette determine field height, vertical position, subject scale, offset, crop, and whether the subject crosses its upper or lower boundary. Do not default to mechanical centring.
- Balance horizontal field, subject mass, positive/negative shape, and upper/lower whitespace as one ratio system. The field relates subject to space and does not become scenery or decoration.
- Preserve generous ivory paper around it. Reject multiple colour panels, borders, patterns, scattered icons, stickers, tape, or repeated motifs.

### Broken wax-pastel friction and paper depth

- Use light-coloured matte wax pastel to establish both mass and essential structure. Marks are loose, irregular, slightly broken, rubbed, and grain-catching, with variable coverage and dry friction against coarse fibre.
- Keep essential contours readable but never mechanically closed or digitally clean. Allow local gaps, doubled touches, flattened edges, and rough abrasion only when they strengthen the hand-drawn rhythm.
- Preserve a subtle double-paper construction: the horizontal field reads as a second low-saturation paper layer on rough ivory handmade stock, with only delicate edge difference and no craft collage clutter.
- Add restrained Risograph grain, tiny registration imperfection, and mild ageing while keeping the work clean. Reject acrylic blocks, oily crayon shine, coloured-pencil hatching, watercolour bloom, ink wash, thick impasto, realistic modelling, smooth digital illustration, and 3D.

### Source-derived low-saturation colour character

- Derive the horizontal field from the source's overall colour and emotion, then lower saturation and unify temperature without erasing its identity.
- Keep the subject primarily light, chalky, and matte; allow only restrained source-derived tonal variation required for recognition. The field remains the main colour event.
- Preserve source warmth/coolness and emotional colour character without reproducing every hue. Never reuse one photograph's field colour for another by default.
- Avoid fixed palettes, rainbow accents, transparent gradients, muddy ageing, neon glow, corporate brand systems, and universal beige filtering.

## Copy and typography belong to 029

029 text behaves like a soft, loose, slightly imperfect handwritten trace within the paper drawing—not a typeset cover line, headline wall, UI label, data display, or detached advertising overlay.

- Automatic copy derives one short line from the source's visible or supported emotion, action, state, relation, or metaphor without inventing a story.
- Prefer one restrained title plus zero to two very small supporting elements. A number, place, or year appears only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 029 construction:

- **Role:** a hand-drawn extension of the subject gesture and horizontal field rhythm.
- **Voice:** quiet, soft, loose, intimate, slightly imperfect, poetic, and restrained.
- **Construction:** one short handwritten line plus at most two much smaller supporting marks when useful. Letterforms remain readable despite natural wobble and uneven baseline.
- **Placement:** follow the field edge, subject gesture, or open paper; allow slight interweave, offset, or boundary crossing without covering defining cues.
- **Material:** soft dry wax-pencil, pastel-pencil, or similarly matte hand mark consistent with the drawing; never glossy digital lettering, formal typesetting, or oversized display type.
- **Script:** use a native relaxed handwritten equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction, joining, direction, and legibility rather than imitating Latin handwriting across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.
